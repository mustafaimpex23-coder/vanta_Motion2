About Us

The studio behind the motion.

We're a team of marketers, designers, editors and developers who believe great brands are built where creativity meets performance.

Our Story

Started small. Built bold.

Vanta Motions began in 2019 as a two-person edit suite making short videos for local businesses. Clients kept asking for more: the ads to run those videos, the designs to match them, the websites to send people to.

So we grew. Today we're a full creative studio covering paid social, design, video, web and search, with one simple promise: every piece of work should look premium and move the numbers.

“We don't just make things look good. We make them work.”

Founder, Vanta Motions
